# The Crystal Palace by Night (E3), 1895: PRIVATE STORY BIBLE

Twist: **no**. Obvious culprit, hard proof. (Recorded in `spoilers/twists.md`.)

Difficulty: a step up from The Operator's Fist, by *depth*: the decisive facts are never
stated; each comes from linking three or four findings. Noise is left in (fireworks
times, train times, red-herring quarrels, the stall-thief theory).

## The crime

- **Victim:** Daniel Pike, 58, night-watchman to the Crystal Palace Company, an old
  soldier (Royal Artillery), careful, obstinate, honest.
- **Culprit:** Mr Bertram Lusk, Superintendent of the Building. He has been cutting the
  published attendance (and so the declared gate takings) for months, pocketing the
  difference with a turnstile clerk long since gone. Pike reads the turnstile counters
  at closing as one of his duties; he kept his own private record and found the gate
  book (entered by Lusk) runs about a tenth under. On Thursday he told Lusk he would go
  to the Secretary on Friday.
- **Means:** the T-headed iron hydrant bar from the glass-fronted fire-hydrant case in
  the Pompeian Court. The cases open only with the fire station's keys (never left
  their hook: Fireman Bowles on duty all night) or the Superintendent's master key on
  his watch-chain.
- **The alibi trick:** Lusk alone keeps the key that opens the watchmen's time detectors
  (portable tell-tale clocks; at each station a chained key, turned in the clock,
  stamps its number and the time on a paper dial). On Thursday morning he sent the
  time-clerk Pidgeon to the stores for fresh dials, which he had never done, and set
  Pike's detector **ten minutes fast**. So Pike's dial shows every keying ten minutes
  later than the truth, and Lusk could be at the Low Level barrier "before" the murder.
- **Body moved:** Lusk killed Pike in the Pompeian Court, as Pike keyed his station there,
  and carried him across the north end into the Alhambra Court, under the gallery,
  to pass for a fall from the gallery. On Friday morning, opening Pike's detector for
  Hopkins, he "obligingly" wound it and set it right, destroying the fast clock; but
  Hopkins kept the dial.

## Lusk's story (lies)

Left his office at 10:25; stopped a minute at the meter house to speak to Wicks about
the night lights; walked the colonnade; 10:42 to Victoria; home at Clapham by 11:30.
Wicks: nobody came near him from 10:25 to 10:40. Sayer: at his barrier from 10:20; Lusk
came through at 10:40, not before, blowing hard, though the colonnade is three minutes
from the office.

## The chains (what the player must link)

1. **The dial is fast by ten minutes.** Pike's dial (key 1 at 10:15) + Crouch's dial in the
   time office (key 1 at 10:05) + Crouch: they always key 1 together. (Key numbers to
   places: key register in the watch-house, or the numbers on the key-boxes.)
2. **He died before the courts went dark.** Pike's lantern never lit + the rule (lanterns
   lit at gas-down, not before; you cannot find the key-boxes in the dark) + Wicks's gas
   book (courts dark 10:33) → he could not have keyed the Pompeian at 10:40 → dial wrong.
   Kell's "just after the half-hour" + the clock is true (Greenwich signal daily).
3. **Only Lusk could set a detector.** Time-office rule card + Pidgeon's Thursday morning.
4. **No fall.** Wound: one narrow blow behind, nothing else broken, palms clean (Watson
   as doctor) + little blood where found + the gallery door locked (and the rule that a
   watchman unlocks going up, locks coming down) + the gallery dust undisturbed, key-box 15
   unturned + no 15 on the dial.
5. **Killed in the Pompeian Court with the hydrant bar.** Last key 7 = Pompeian + blood in
   the mosaic joints + empty hydrant case, glass whole + bar in the impluvium matches the
   wound + Rudd: cases full at 9:30, keys never off the hook, Super's master key.
6. **Lusk's alibi is a lie.** True death ~10:31; Lusk at barrier 10:40; the walk (Holmes:
   8-9 minutes from the north end); his 10:25 departure and visit to Wicks are false.
7. **Motive.** Pike's notebook (locker; needs his keys from his effects) gives Thursday's
   counters as 30,912; the attendance board and gate book say 27,840.

## Final (accuse) answers

who = lusk; where = pompeian; when = half_past (about 10:31); trick = fast (detector set
ten minutes fast); weapon = bar; why = figures.

## Red herrings / noise

- Kell (organ tuner): fined on Pike's report for smoking in the organ. In the loft all
  evening; heard the cry; didn't go down.
- Hatch (Brock's foreman): words with Pike about frames on the terrace walk. In the
  grounds with Rudd till 10:45.
- Hopkins's theories: accident (police surgeon: a fall, "about eleven"), or a stall thief
  from the firework crowd (a jeweller's shutter in the south nave scratched).
- Fireworks firing log, train times, Crouch's south round.

## The round mechanic (Friday night)

Crouch walks both rounds tonight. Stops: centre 0-3, north end 4-7, Pompeian 8-11,
Alhambra 12-15 (gallery stair unlocked while he is there), south nave 16-19, south
transept 20-23, watch-house 24-31 (tea). Counter `round` starts at 24. He does not move on
while Watson is with him. STATUS shows where he is.
