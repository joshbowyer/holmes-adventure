# The Margaret Ainsley in Dry Dock (E5), 1897: PRIVATE STORY BIBLE

Twist: **YES**. Recorded in `spoilers/twists.md`. Never tell the user.

- Obvious suspect: **Mick Laverty**, boatswain, paid off Monday. He quarrelled with the mate
  at sea, threatened him at the pay table ("I'll see you in the dock, Mister"), came
  aboard drunk on Thursday night (11:20 to 11:50, in the watchman's book), and pawned the
  mate's gold watch on Friday morning. Lestrade has him.
- Real culprit: **Captain Gideon Haldane**, marine superintendent of the owners, Tolland &
  Marr. Obviously innocent: he left the ship at 6:10 p.m. (watchman's book), went home to
  Greenwich, and "the gangway is the only way aboard". Helpful, grave, pressing the case
  against Laverty.
- **Lynchpin**: Haldane's grey tweed overcoat, rolled up and pushed into the coal box in the
  owners' hut: red lead on both cuffs and the knees, and a three-cornered tear in the
  right cuff. A shred of grey tweed is caught on a burr at the edge of the cut-away
  plating under No. 1 hold. Red lead was laid on those edges at 5:30 p.m. Thursday, wet
  twelve hours: so whoever brushed them did it that night.

Difficulty: a step above the Wax Cylinder, and per the user's last note **no single insight
unlocks the end**. Three independent strands must each be worked: WHEN (clears Laverty),
HOW (the route through the bottom), WHO (Haldane); plus WHY (the scrap log).

## The facts

- s.s. *Margaret Ainsley*, iron screw steamer, 1,900 tons, of Tolland & Marr, Fenchurch
  Street. Home from Rosario with maize. On **9 April 1897** at 3:10 a.m. she went on the
  Ortiz Bank in the River Plate, got off, came home leaking. Insured for £28,000; worth
  perhaps £15,000 (Dalziel). The grounding was **deliberate**: the owners, through
  Haldane, had the master put her on the bank, meaning her to be lost. She was not.
- Crew paid off **Monday 14 June** (Jubilee week; the Jubilee is Tuesday 22nd). Into
  **Pellew's Dry Dock, Rotherhithe** on Tuesday 15th for Lloyd's survey. Board of Trade
  inquiry into the grounding opens **Monday 21st**.
- **Robert Fairlie**, chief officer, 34, sober, exact. He kept his own private log, a black
  book, with the true courses at the grounding: the master's order "S 62 W" straight on
  to the bank. He told Haldane he would put it before the inquiry unless paid £500.
- **Captain Silas Wedderburn**, master, 55, drinks; complicit in the fraud; innocent of
  the murder (drunk asleep in his cabin from 9 p.m.; Pennock sat up in the pantry across
  his door till 2).

## The strands

1. **WHEN (clears Laverty of the killing)**: Kerr's book (Laverty off at 11:50; nobody else
   by the gangway till six) + Pennock (cocoa at 12:30) + Kerr (lantern forward before one)
   + the surgeon via Lestrade (died between midnight and two). Laverty only stole the
   watch: it hung in the cabin (Quayle: the mate hung it on a hook when writing), and
   Laverty's own story.
2. **WHERE / HOW**: the mate's lantern burned dry and the cocoa can on the tank top of No. 1
   hold + Lindqvist ("you can walk into her from the dock bottom through number one now") +
   the opening (red lead, the scrape of something dragged) + red lead on the back of the
   mate's coat and his heels, none on his hands or knees (Watson) + Sowerby's paint book
   (red lead 5:30 p.m.) + forecastle head: rails down but the dust on the deck edge
   undisturbed, rope still across. Not a fall: killed in the hold, carried out the bottom.
3. **WHO**: only someone off the ship could use that route unseen; the dock gate after 10
   needs a pass-key (Jessop's register: four holders) + the shred of grey tweed on the burr
   + Haldane's coat in the coal box (lynchpin) + Haldane lied (home by 11). The other
   key-holders: dockmaster (in Kent all week), Sowerby (home in Deptford, wife and six
   children; his key on his watch-chain; no tweed coat), Dalziel (at Lloyd's Register
   dinner, then Liverpool by the night mail).
4. **WHY**: Quayle (the mate's black book: "worth more than the ship"); the drawer in the
   mate's cabin empty; the chart in the chartroom with a pencilled course rubbed out but
   its indentation still showing S 62 W straight on the bank; Dalziel: damage from driving
   on at speed in slack water, and the insurance; Wedderburn evasive.
