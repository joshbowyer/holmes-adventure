For later cases, decide when writing the bible and record it here and in the bible.
Sketch for a twist case: the suspect the evidence points at has motive, means, a broken
alibi and a damning physical clue that turns out to have an innocent explanation. The
real culprit's innocence rests on one assumption (a time, an identity, a capability)
that the lynchpin clue breaks. The lynchpin must be findable without hints, needed for
the good ending, and its absence must lead to a convincing wrong ending, not a dead end.
